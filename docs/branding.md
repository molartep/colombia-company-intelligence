## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: standalone transparent PNG logo for the header of Colombia Company Intelligence, an open-source company research application. Primary request: a recognizable geographically faithful outline/silhouette of mainland Colombia constructed entirely from a connected network of circular dots of different sizes. Dots use Colombian flag yellow (#FCD116), royal blue (#003893), and red (#CE1126), with crisp thin white edges around each dot. Use yellow in the northern/top region, blue in the central region, red in the southern/bottom region. Thin matching-colored connecting lines between neighboring dots create an elegant network inside the country silhouette. Preserve Colombia's recognizable outline including the northeastern La Guajira peninsula, northwestern Panama-side indentation, western Pacific coast and southeastern Amazonas point. Style: clean flat professional logo, balanced node sizes, sharp edges, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D. Composition: one centered mark filling most of a square canvas with a small even safety margin, visually legible as a header logo around 120 pixels tall. Background: genuinely transparent with alpha, no backdrop or frame. Text: none. No letters, no map labels, no surrounding countries, no extra icons, no watermark.
